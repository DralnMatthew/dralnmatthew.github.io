Research

My research develops amortized techniques to make Bayesian inference, experimental design, and optimization fast enough to run in the loop.

Research interests

Amortized Inference
Networks trained once that infer for new problems in a single pass.
Bayesian Experimental Design
Choosing informative experiments adaptively, fast enough to run in the loop.
Bayesian Optimization
Sample-efficient optimization of expensive black-box objectives.
